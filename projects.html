desktop_audio_software

ALL Speakers Studio

$ download --app ↗

ALL Speakers Studio is a Windows-based real-time multi-device audio broadcasting application developed by ABTS Technologies. It allows users to broadcast computer audio simultaneously to multiple connected audio output devices, including internal speakers, Bluetooth speakers and TWS earbuds, wired headphones, USB audio devices, soundbars, and HDMI/DisplayPort audio outputs.

The application automatically discovers connected audio devices and provides individual speaker selection, master volume control, individual volume adjustment, one-click master mute, device rescanning, and automatic hot-plug detection. Users can also configure synchronization offsets to compensate for the inherent latency of Bluetooth audio devices.

ALL Speakers Studio uses Windows native audio routing technology and is designed to operate without requiring third-party virtual audio cables, external drivers, or additional plugins. It also supports both a standard Windows installation and a portable execution mode.
